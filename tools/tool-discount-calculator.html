Discount Calculator

The Discount Calculator takes an original price and a discount percentage and instantly shows the amount you save along with the final sale price, making it simple to check whether a deal is as good as it looks. It's useful while shopping in-store or online, comparing coupon codes, or working out markdowns for a small business.

Every figure is calculated live in your browser as you type, with no server processing or data upload involved at any point. That means you can quickly test several discount percentages back to back and see the sale price update instantly each time.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Open the Discount Calculator tool.
  2. Enter the original price of the item.
  3. Enter the discount percentage being offered.
  4. View the amount saved and the final sale price, updated instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the sale price calculated?

The tool multiplies the original price by the discount percentage to find the savings, then subtracts that from the original price to give the final sale price.

Can I stack multiple discounts?

You can apply the calculator more than once, using the result of one discount as the starting price for the next, since successive discounts are not simply additive.

Does this include sales tax?

No, this calculator only handles the discount itself; use the GST or Sales Tax Calculator separately if you also need to add tax to the discounted price.
